Multi-agent trajectory forecasting is widely studied in pedestrian crowds, robotics, and autonomous driving. Team sports introduce a structured setting where motion is shaped by continuous dynamics, role heterogeneity across the ball and opposing teams, and coordinated group behaviors that evolve with the spatial context of a play. We propose H3M, a hierarchical multi-scale motion generative framework that integrates (i) an Individual-level Dynamics Module for dynamics-consistent motion evolution via low-rank dynamics bases, (ii) a Role-adaptive Behavior Module using mixture-of experts routing for role-conditioned representations, and (iii) a Group Relational Decoder with spatially modulated relational reasoning for coordinating multiple agents. Experiments on NFL and NBA SportVU benchmarks, evaluated under standard multimodal metrics, demonstrate superior performance, with additional analyses revealing the contributions of these three hierarchical modules.
